How a problem is fixed

The operations agent proposes; only you approve. It can restart or resize a service, never change what it runs.

  1. Observereads service state
  2. Diagnosefixed rules, no AI
  3. Proposerestart or bounded resize
  4. Your approvalvalid once, for 5 minutes
  5. Execute onceonly if nothing changed
  6. Verifyhealthy again, or reported
